# FreshProof Product Principles

1. **Evidence before verdicts.** Retrieval is not verification; sources must be inspected and compared.
2. **Claim-level output.** Never assign one blanket freshness score to an entire answer.
3. **Separate dimensions.** Freshness, source quality, corroboration, and confidence must not be conflated.
4. **Calibrated abstention.** When evidence is absent, ambiguous, or conflicting, say so.
5. **Temporal semantics.** Distinguish the date an event occurred from the date a page was published or updated.
6. **Source provenance.** Every evidence item must preserve its source URL and retrieval metadata.
7. **Quota safety.** Bound input size, claims per answer, and results per claim; avoid automatic paid overages.
8. **Privacy by default.** Do not persist submitted text; do not log request bodies or secrets.
9. **No fabricated metrics.** Publish benchmark results only after reproducible tests on a labeled dataset.
10. **Human-readable explanations.** Explain why a claim needs review and what evidence was found.
